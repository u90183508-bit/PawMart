Pet Care Services

PawMart offers affordable veterinary and grooming services for your pets.

General Check-Up

Our veterinarian will check your pet’s weight, temperature, skin, eyes, ears, and overall condition. A check-up is recommended if your pet has vomiting, diarrhea, loss of appetite, or unusual behavior.

Price: ₱350

Vaccination

We provide vaccines for dogs and cats to help protect them from rabies, distemper, parvovirus, and other diseases. Puppies and kittens may need several doses during their first months.

Price: ₱800 – ₱1,500

Deworming

Deworming helps remove intestinal parasites that can cause weight loss, vomiting, diarrhea, and poor growth. It is especially important for puppies and kittens.

Price: ₱250 – ₱500

Pet Grooming

Grooming includes bathing, blow-drying, hair trimming, nail clipping, and ear cleaning. Regular grooming helps prevent skin problems and keeps pets comfortable.

Price: ₱400 – ₱900
