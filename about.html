About PawMart

PawMart is an online pet shop and veterinary clinic that provides affordable pet products and basic animal healthcare services. We started in 2025 as a small store selling dog and cat food. Today, we also offer pet medicine, vitamins, grooming products, toys, and accessories.

Our team believes that pets are part of the family. That is why we make sure that every product we sell is safe, useful, and suitable for everyday pet care. We also provide veterinary services to help pet owners monitor and protect their pet’s health.

Our Mission

To provide affordable and reliable pet products while offering quality veterinary care for dogs, cats, and other small pets.

Our Vision

To become a trusted one-stop pet shop and clinic where pet owners can conveniently buy products and access essential pet care services.
